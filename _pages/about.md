Hello! I am Mutian Wang (王牧天). I am a graduate student in Astronomy at Nanjing University, China. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Nanjing university. During Nov. 2024 - Nov. 2025, I was a visiting student at the Institute for Astronomy at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa.

By combining observations from space- and ground-based facilities with numerical simulations and theoretical groundings, my research focuses on the orbital architectures of young and near-resonant planetary systems, as well as circumbinary planets. I aim to understand how planetary systems acquire, change, or preserve their orbital configurations across different evolutionary stages. Besides, I also studied young star accretion.
